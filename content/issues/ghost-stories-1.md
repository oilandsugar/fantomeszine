Ghost Stories is a off-issue zine put together by Sarsen, who collected ghost stories for 5 years before combining them with some of his photogaphy. The stories range from personal ghostly experiences, cryptid sightings, and various hauntings told from the perspective of those who lived them.

Featuring stories, drawings, and photographs by Soren, Salem, Chelsie, Jack, Kylie, Marylin, and Mars.  

Layout and typography by Lizbeth Poirier
